What the comparison does

Quantities you can trace.

Selected PDF drawings and agreed measurement rules are used to prepare a draft Bill of Quantities. Each line is set alongside the approved historical takeoff for a documented review.

A traceable working format

Every line remains reviewable.

Stable item identifiers and drawing, page and revision references make the draft reviewable. Dimensions, scale uncertainty and drawing conflicts are flagged for reviewer attention; they are not silently inferred.

  • Draft BOQ supplied in XLSX or CSV format
  • Source drawing register and agreed rules included
  • Original and corrected quantities kept separate

The back-test record

A comparison pack—not just a quantity list.

Designed to answer whether this trade justifies further validation.

  1. 01

    Draft BOQ and source trail

    A draft XLSX or CSV Bill of Quantities with stable item ID, description, unit, quantity and drawing/page/revision references; plus a source drawing register.

  2. 02

    Rules, assumptions and exceptions

    The agreed measurement rules, assumptions and exceptions are recorded. Unclear dimensions, scale and conflicts are flagged rather than guessed.

  3. 03

    Line-by-line reconciliation

    Comparison against the approved historical takeoff: matched, missing, extra, duplicate and disputed items; original and corrected quantities kept separately; and reviewer disposition.

  4. 04

    Effort and decision report

    A human-effort assessment covering preparation, review, correction, rework, administration and fallback—with client and ETHINX hours shown separately—and a proceed, narrow or stop recommendation.

  5. 05

    Review meeting and factual corrections

    A 60-minute review meeting, then one consolidated factual-correction round if submitted within five business days of delivery under the signed offer.

Discuss the fit ↓
Does this replace our current estimating process?

No. The historical back-test is a controlled comparison alongside an approved reference takeoff. Any later pilot keeps the existing takeoff process in place. Outputs remain draft and are not for tender use until every final line has been approved by the client reviewer.

Do you guarantee accuracy or time savings?

No. The purpose of the paid back-test is to measure a defined scope against an approved historical reference and document where it matched, differed, needed correction or should not proceed. No accuracy, turnaround or labour-saving outcome is guaranteed.

What makes a suitable historical back-test scope?

It is one completed representative project, one trade, with no more than 40 relevant PDF drawing pages and 150 reference BOQ lines in total. The exact approved drawing revision must match the reference BOQ. Clear units, descriptions and measurement rules are required.

What reference material is required?

Start with the project type, trade, drawing revision, approximate page and line counts, and reviewer availability—not confidential files. After scope and handling are approved, provide the aligned approved reference BOQ in XLSX/CSV, matching drawings and measurement rules. You must have rights to share the materials.

Can we use a completed project rather than as-built records?

Yes. The comparison is against the approved historical takeoff and matching drawing revision, not an as-built verification exercise. It does not verify design, engineering, site conditions or procurement.

How much reviewer time is involved?

Your reviewer confirms scope and rules, checks the quantity comparison and joins the 60-minute findings meeting. Line-review and correction time are additional and agreed during scoping; the meeting is not the entire review commitment. Client and ETHINX hours are assessed separately.

How is the historical back-test invoiced?

The price is AUD $2,500, with GST added only where applicable. ETHINX issues a manual invoice after scope approval. The target delivery is 10 business days from payment, complete inputs, written scope approval and data approval; it is not a timer from an enquiry.

Is there a pilot after the historical back-test?

Only where the historical evidence is positive or explicitly qualified, and only on the same validated trade. A separate four-week shadow-mode pilot may cover up to three live tender packages, combined maximum 120 relevant PDF pages and 450 draft BOQ lines, with one agreed revision per package. It is separately scoped and priced; there is no obligation to proceed.
